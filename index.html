Get to know me

I'm John-Rey, a recent Information Technology graduate with a genuine passion for building things that live on the internet. From clean, functional websites to full-scale applications, I enjoy turning ideas into digital solutions that are both practical and pleasant to use.

My journey into tech started with curiosity and has grown into a full-blown dedication to writing clean code, designing intuitive interfaces, and continuously learning new tools and frameworks.
